Week 5: Selective Repeat ARQ Packet-Loss Experiment

Setup:
- Sender sends packets to UDP relay on port 9001.
- UDP relay forwards packets to receiver on port 9000.
- First DATA packet is deliberately dropped by the relay.

Observed results:
- Relay dropped the first DATA packet.
- Sender retransmitted DATA seq=0 after timeout.
- Receiver acknowledged and delivered DATA seq=0.
- Sender received the ACK and sent FIN.
- cmp data/test.txt received_sr.txt returned no differences.

Conclusion:
Selective Repeat successfully recovered from simulated packet loss
through timeout-based retransmission of the missing packet.
